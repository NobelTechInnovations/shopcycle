# Oyklane brand guidelines

The source of truth for how Oyklane looks and sounds on the marketing site
(oyklane.com), the Oyklane Store (market.oyklane.com) and sign-in pages. The
seller dashboard follows the same colours with its own UI font (Inter).

## Who we are

Oyklane is the online-store platform for Indian brands: a store designed
without code, One-Click Checkout, UPI, cards and cash on delivery, GST
invoices, WhatsApp and SMS, Meta and Google ads from the dashboard, and an
app and theme marketplace (the Oyklane Store).

**Promise:** your own online store, made for selling in India.

## Personality and voice

| Trait | We sound like | We don't sound like |
|---|---|---|
| Clear | "Take UPI, cards and cash on delivery." | "Unlock omnichannel payment synergies." |
| Dependable | "Payouts go straight to your gateway account." | "Revolutionary, blazing-fast payouts!" |
| Local | "GST invoices with HSN codes and your buyer's GSTIN." | Generic global copy with $ prices |
| Helpful | "Stuck? Ask in Hinglish." | "Contact our support team for assistance." |

- Plain words, short sentences, sentence case. Prices in ₹, plus GST.
- Say what a button does: "Start free trial", "See pricing", "Visit the Oyklane Store".
- No hype words (revolutionary, seamless, unlock, supercharge), no exclamation marks in headings.

## Colour

Inspired by Microsoft's calm blue: one blue for actions, sky washes for
sections, navy for the dark surfaces.

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Oyklane Blue | `#0067B8` | Primary buttons, links, icons, focus — the one accent |
| Blue Deep | `#005A9E` | Hover / pressed, link text on tints |
| Sky Mist | `#E5F1FB` | Icon tiles, selected rows, highlighted cards |
| Sky Wash | `#EEF6FD` → `#E3F0FB` | Alternate sections and the hero background |
| Sky | `#2B9FE6` | The logo gradient's second colour, charts |
| Navy | `#0F2A4A` | Strengths band, sign-in panel, super-admin sidebar |
| Ink | `#111827` | Headings and body text |
| Slate | `#4B5563` | Secondary text |
| Line | `#E1E8F0` | Borders and dividers |

Text contrast is at least 4.5:1 (AA). Gradients: only the logo mark (blue → sky)
and the soft sky wash behind the hero.

## Typography

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** (Google Fonts) for the marketing site, weights 400 / 500 / 600 / 700 / 800.
- Product UI (dashboard): Inter.

| Role | Size | Weight | Line height | Tracking |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display (hero) | 44–64px | 800 | 1.05 | -0.035em |
| H2 (section) | 30–44px | 700 | 1.12 | -0.03em |
| H3 (card) | 19–22px | 700 | 1.3 | -0.01em |
| Lead | 18–20px | 400 | 1.6 | 0 |
| Body | 16–17px | 400 | 1.65 | 0 |
| Small / meta | 13–14px | 500 | 1.5 | 0 |

Lines stay under ~70 characters. Headings are one colour — no highlighted words.

## Icons

- Outline icons on a 24px grid, 1.8px stroke, round caps (the site's `Icon` set).
- In features they sit in a 46px **icon tile**: Sky Mist square, 12px radius, blue icon.
  On the navy band: white 8% tile, light-sky icon (`#9FD3FF`).
- Brand logos (Razorpay, Meta, Google, WhatsApp…) always in their own colours on a white tile.

## Layout

- Full-width bands, content up to 1320px wide, 32px side padding (16–20px on phones).
- Section rhythm: 112px top and bottom (80px on phones); alternate white and Sky Wash; one
  Navy band for strengths; a blue band for the closing call to action.
- Radius: 12px buttons and tiles, 16–20px cards and product frames.
- Shadows only under product visuals and floating cards, never under plain cards.
- Motion: one moving part per screen (the product tour); respect reduced motion.

## Logo

- The mark (blue → sky rounded square with a white ring) plus the word "Oyklane" in 700–800.
- Clear space: the mark's height on every side. Minimum mark size 24px.
- Don't recolour, stretch, add shadows or put it on busy photos. On Navy use the
  white wordmark.
